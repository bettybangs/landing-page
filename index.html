I help growing companies get ready for their SOC 2 audit: scoped correctly, documented, and able to show evidence before fieldwork begins.

How I get a client audit-ready

  1. ScopeDefine system boundaries and select the Trust Services Criteria that fit the business.
  2. AssessRun a gap assessment against those criteria and build a prioritized risk register.
  3. DocumentDraft policies and control descriptions the team can actually operate.
  4. Collect evidenceSet up evidence collection, including automated checks that pull proof straight from source systems.
  5. Hand offDeliver an organized, audit-ready package to the client and their audit firm.

Working alongside audit firms

I do readiness, not attestation. Your firm keeps the audit and its independence; I prepare the client so they arrive organized, with fewer surprises and less back-and-forth during fieldwork.
